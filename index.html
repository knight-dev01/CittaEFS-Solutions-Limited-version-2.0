Core Products & Enterprise Solutions

1. CittaEFS (Intelligent Electronic Fiscal System & Regulatory Middleware)

CittaEFS bridges enterprise ledger billing to the National Revenue Service (NRS) for instant, automated fiscal pre-clearance with sub-240ms latency. Provides SHA-256 cryptographic invoice signing, decimal precision validation, and an immutable 7-year audit vault. Fully compatible with SAP S/4HANA, Oracle NetSuite, Odoo, and Microsoft Dynamics 365 without requiring core software changes.

2. CittaMatrix (Enterprise ERP & General Ledger)

High-velocity general ledger, multi-currency transaction routing, automated bank reconciliations, inventory management, and procurement workflows for multi-entity corporate structures.

3. CittaHospitalityX (Hotel PMS & Guest Experience Suite)

End-to-end Property Management System for luxury hotels, boutique resorts, and serviced apartments, featuring automated housekeeping schedules, multi-point folio billing, and keyless check-in coordination.

4. CittaPlannerX (Interactive Gantt & Resource Scheduler)

Visual resource planning canvas with critical path analysis, drag-and-drop dependency management, and equipment capacity leveling for heavy engineering, logistics, and construction enterprises.

5. CittaNexus (Enterprise API Gateway & Mesh)

Microsecond API router with cryptographic payload signing, token vaults, and circuit breaker fault-tolerance for distributed enterprise services.
